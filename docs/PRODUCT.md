# Product Brief: Adaptive Running Coach

## 1. Overview
Adaptive Running Coach is an online platform that helps runners intelligently adjust their current training plans based on recent performance and health data. By analyzing the user’s past runs and metrics (such as distance, heart rate, sleep, et ), the platform provides data-driven recommendations to optimize their weekly training.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Runners often follow static training plans that don’t adapt to their real-world performance, recovery, or life changes. This leads to overtraining, stagnation, or injuries. There’s a need for a simple, personalized system that fine-tunes existing plans based on how the athlete is actually performing.

---

## 3. Goal
Enable runners to **dynamically adjust their training plans** using their **past month’s fitness data** to achieve sustainable progress without burnout or injury.

---

## 4. Target Audience
- Beginner to semi-professional runners  
- Runners who are following a structured training plan
- Users who log their runs on **Strava**

---

## 5. Key Features

### a. Strava Integration
- Users log in using their Strava account.  
- The system fetches the past 1 month of running data, including:  
  - Distance ran  
  - Average and max heart rate  
  - Sleep score (if available)  
  - Frequency and duration of runs
  - etc

### b. Training Plan Input
- Users upload or define their current training plan.  
- The platform identifies the current week of training to provide week-specific tweaks.

### c. Personalized Recommendation
- Upon first login, users receive a **default recommendation** for their current week based on their data and plan.  
- Users can **regenerate** a new recommendation if unsatisfied with the default one.


---

## 6. Success Metrics
- % of users who complete onboarding (Strava login + plan upload)  
- Engagement: average number of recommendations generated per user  
- Retention: % of users returning weekly to check updated plans  
- User satisfaction (NPS or qualitative feedback)

---

## 7. Out of Scope (for v1)
- Multi-source health data integrations (Garmin, Fitbit, etc.)  
- Nutrition or recovery recommendations  
- Long-term adaptive plan generation (beyond two weeks)  
- Mobile app (web-first MVP)
