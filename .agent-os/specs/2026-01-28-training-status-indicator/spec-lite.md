# Spec Summary (Lite)

Implement an AI-powered training status indicator that appears prominently at the top of the dashboard, showing users whether their training is "On Track", "Slightly Off Track", or "Off Track" with a brief explanation. The feature uses LLM analysis of the user's training plan, last two weeks of Strava activities, current training week, and running experience to provide a 90%+ confidence status assessment, available from week 2 onwards.
