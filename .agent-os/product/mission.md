# Product Mission

## Pitch

Adaptive Training Plan is an online platform that helps runners intelligently adjust their training plans based on recent performance and health data by analyzing Strava metrics and providing AI-powered weekly recommendations.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Amateur to Semi-Professional Runners**: Athletes who follow structured training plans and want to optimize their performance while avoiding injury and burnout.
- **Strava Users**: Runners who already track their workouts on Strava and want actionable insights from their data.

### User Personas

**Sarah, the Marathon Trainee** (28-35 years old)
- **Role:** Marketing Manager training for her first marathon
- **Context:** Follows a 16-week marathon training plan but struggles to adjust when life gets busy or when she's fatigued
- **Pain Points:** Static training plans don't account for poor sleep, high stress weeks, or unexpected schedule changes; unsure when to push harder or ease back
- **Goals:** Complete marathon without injury, maintain training consistency, understand when to modify workouts based on recovery data

**Mike, the Competitive Runner** (30-45 years old)
- **Role:** Software Engineer competing in local 5K/10K races
- **Context:** Uses structured training plans to improve race times but wants more personalized adjustments
- **Pain Points:** Training plans don't adapt to actual performance data, overtraining leads to plateaus or injuries, difficult to know if he's progressing optimally
- **Goals:** Achieve new personal records, train smarter not just harder, prevent overtraining and injuries

## The Problem

### Static Training Plans Don't Adapt to Reality

Runners follow rigid training schedules that ignore real-world performance, recovery, and life circumstances. This leads to overtraining, injury risk, motivation loss, and suboptimal results. Studies show that 50-70% of runners experience overuse injuries annually.

**Our Solution:** Adaptive Training Plan analyzes your past month of Strava data (distance, heart rate, sleep, frequency) and intelligently adjusts your current week's training to match your actual fitness level and recovery state.

### No Personalized Guidance Without a Coach

Hiring a running coach costs $100-$300/month and requires ongoing communication. Most runners can't afford or don't need full coaching but still want expert-level plan adjustments based on their data.

**Our Solution:** We provide AI-powered coaching recommendations at a fraction of the cost, giving runners data-driven adjustments to their training plans whenever they need them.

### Data Overload Without Actionable Insights

Strava provides extensive data but no guidance on how to adjust training based on that data. Runners see their metrics but don't know what they mean for next week's workouts.

**Our Solution:** We translate Strava metrics into concrete training recommendations: "Reduce this week's long run by 2 miles" or "Add an extra rest day based on elevated heart rate trends."

## Differentiators

### AI-Powered Plan Adaptation

Unlike static PDF training plans or generic calendar apps, we provide dynamic, personalized adjustments based on actual performance data. This results in safer training progression and better race outcomes by preventing overtraining and optimizing recovery.

### Strava-First Integration

Unlike comprehensive training platforms like TrainingPeaks (which require manual data entry and cost $20+/month), we focus exclusively on Strava users and provide instant value from existing data. This results in zero-friction onboarding and immediate personalized recommendations without changing existing workflows.

### Recommendation Regeneration

Unlike one-time analysis tools, we allow users to regenerate recommendations if they're unsatisfied with the initial suggestion. This provides flexibility and user control while maintaining data-driven guidance, resulting in higher user satisfaction and engagement.


### User Experience Features

- **Default Recommendation on Login:** Provides immediate value with an automatic recommendation upon first login.
- **Recommendation Regeneration:** Allows users to request alternative recommendations if unsatisfied with initial guidance.
- **Week-Specific Guidance:** Tailors recommendations to specific week of training plan (e.g., taper week vs. build week).
- **Plain-Language Explanations:** Translates data insights into clear, actionable workout modifications with reasoning.
