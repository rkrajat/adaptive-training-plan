# Product Mission (Lite)

Adaptive Training Plan is an online platform that helps runners intelligently adjust their training plans based on recent performance and health data by analyzing Strava metrics and providing AI-powered weekly recommendations.

Adaptive Training Plan serves amateur to semi-professional runners who follow structured training plans and use Strava to track workouts. Unlike static PDF training plans or manual coaching, we automatically analyze 30 days of Strava data (distance, heart rate, sleep) and provide dynamic, personalized weekly training adjustments to prevent overtraining, optimize recovery, and improve race outcomes.
